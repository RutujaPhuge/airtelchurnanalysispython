# Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

# Key Insights 

- 26.54% of customers have Churned out.
- Significant number of people in senior citizen category have churned out.
- People who have month to month contract are likely to churn then from those who have 1 or 2 years contract.
- Maximum churning is in Phone service
